## Current cohort

## Schedule

Sessions run on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 1:00 PM to 3:30 PM Eastern Time.

There are two sessions each week, roughly two and a half hours each, over ten
weeks. Employees must be paid their regular wages while attending, which is a
Workforce Training Fund requirement.

Cohorts are capped at 15 companies.

should be ignored.

Any end date of April 1, 2027 for the Winter cohort is incorrect. The correct end
date is March 25, 2027.
